# Student Guide

Welcome to Cybersecurity-Academy. This guide explains how to learn on the
platform, take labs and exams, and earn and share verifiable certificates.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. **Register** with your e-mail and a strong password, then **verify your
   e-mail** from the link you receive. Verification is required before some
   actions.
2. **Choose your language.** The platform is fully available in **English** and
   **French**; switch any time from the language selector. If a particular piece
   of content has not been fully translated yet, you will be told — you will
   never be shown English quietly in place of French.
3. **Pick a path:** SOC Analysis, Penetration Testing, or Governance, Risk &
   Compliance (GRC). You can explore the syllabus before enrolling.

---

## 2. How courses are organised

```
Path  →  Course  →  Module  →  Lessons · Labs · Quiz
```

A course groups modules; each module has lessons, may include labs, and ends
with a quiz. A final exam covers the whole course. Some modules are marked **in
development** — their lessons are still being written and reviewed, and will
appear when ready.

---

## 3. Lessons

Each lesson states its **objectives**, explains the topic with worked examples,
and usually includes:

- **Scenarios** — realistic situations to reason through.
- **Exercises** with **solutions** you can reveal to self-check.
- **Hints** when you get stuck.
- An **assignment** and the **criteria** that describe good work.
- **References** for going deeper.

Your progress is saved as you go. Work through a module's lessons before its
quiz.

---

## 4. Labs

Labs are hands-on: you examine a **synthetic dataset** (for example
authentication logs or network flows) and answer a series of **tasks**.

- Download the dataset where provided and analyse it with the tools the lesson
  suggests.
- Submit each task's answer; **scoring happens on the server** and you get
  immediate feedback.
- Labs can be retried so you can learn from mistakes.

### Offensive labs and safety

Penetration-testing labs teach techniques for **authorised** testing only.
Targets are intentionally vulnerable applications that **you deploy in your own
isolated environment** — the platform does not host targets and does not attack
anything. Never use these techniques against systems you do not own or do not
have explicit written permission to test. Read the in-app Responsible Use and
Lab Safety Policy.

---

## 5. Quizzes and the final exam

- **Module quizzes** check one module.
- The **final exam** draws questions at random and runs under a **timer shown on
  screen**. Once started, your questions are fixed for that attempt.
- Answer every question before time runs out; you can review your answers
  afterwards. Retake rules depend on the assessment.

All scoring is done on the server, so your result is authoritative.

---

## 6. Certificates

When you complete a course — its lessons, labs and the final exam — a
certificate is issued **automatically**.

- Each certificate has a **unique number** and can be checked on the **public
  verification page**, which shows whether it is valid, revoked, or has been
  tampered with.
- You can download it as a **PDF** (available in English and French) with a QR
  code that links to verification.

> **Important:** Cybersecurity-Academy certificates recognise what you achieved
> **on this platform**. They are **not** accredited, government-recognised, or
> equivalent to external professional certifications, and should not be
> presented as such.

---

## 7. Sharing (optional)

Sharing is entirely **opt-in** — nothing about your learning is public unless
you choose to share it.

- Share a certificate to **LinkedIn** or **Facebook** via official share links.
- For **TikTok**, download the certificate image and copy the link to post
  manually.

The platform never posts on your behalf and never publishes your certificates
automatically.

---

## 8. Your account

- Update your profile and **language** from account settings.
- **Change your password** at any time — doing so signs out your other
  sessions for safety.
- Review your enrolments, progress and certificates from your dashboard.

---

## 9. Getting help

Use the **contact page** for questions or problems. If you believe you have
found a security issue, use the **Security** category. Be respectful of other
learners and never attempt to access data that is not yours.

Good luck with your studies.
